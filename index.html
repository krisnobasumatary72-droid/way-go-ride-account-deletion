Account Deletion Request

Users of WAY GO RIDE can request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: Through the App

Open WAY GO RIDE, go to your Profile section and select the Delete Account option. Follow the instructions shown in the app.

Option 2: By Email

If you cannot access the app, send an account deletion request from your registered email address. Include your registered phone number so that we can identify your account.

Request Account Deletion

Data Deletion

After verifying your request, we will process the deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to applicable legal and regulatory retention requirements.

Certain transaction or payment records may need to be retained where required by law.
